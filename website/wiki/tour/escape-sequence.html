Escape Sequence

written by Pouya Mohammadi

In the name of Allah, the Merciful.

Ever features custom escape sequences that may not exist in other languages. These sequences are primarily designed to facilitate the compilation process, ensuring that stages such as lexing or tokenization proceed without issues; consequently, some of them may be removed in future versions of the language.
A table of the supported escape sequences is provided below.

Escape sequence Equal to
\b * " (like \" in C-style languages)
\p * (
\cp * )

* "cp" stands for close parenthesis.

* "p" stands for parenthesis.

* "b" was chosen arbitrarily and is not an abbreviation for anything.
